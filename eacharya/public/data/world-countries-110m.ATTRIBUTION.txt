world-countries-110m.geojson

Source: Natural Earth, 1:110m Cultural Vectors, Admin 0 - Countries (v5.x),
        https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/110m-cultural-vectors/
        redistributed at https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector
Licence: Public domain. "Made with Natural Earth. Free vector and raster map data @ naturalearthdata.com."

Modifications (see scripts/build-boundaries.mjs): kept a subset of properties, rounded coordinates to
3 decimals, and added "alat"/"alng", the centroid of each country's largest polygon, used only as an
approximate anchor for proportional symbols. Boundary geometry is otherwise unchanged.
"pop" / "popYear" are Natural Earth's POP_EST / POP_YEAR: approximate estimates, not official statistics.

Natural Earth shows de facto boundaries at a small scale. Boundaries (including disputed areas) may differ
from those officially recognised by any government. Not for navigation, legal or official use.
